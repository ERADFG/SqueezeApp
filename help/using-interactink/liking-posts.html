Liking posts

Article 8 of 22 in Using InteractInk

Tap the heart icon under any post to like it — a quick, low-friction way to show appreciation without needing to leave a public reply, while still letting the author know their post landed with someone.

Liked posts can influence what shows up in your For You feed, since InteractInk treats your likes as one signal, among others, for figuring out what kind of content you're generally interested in seeing more of. Your list of liked posts is visible from your own profile by default, though you can set it to private in your settings if you'd rather keep what you like to yourself. You can remove a like at any time simply by tapping the heart icon on that post again, which reverses it instantly.
