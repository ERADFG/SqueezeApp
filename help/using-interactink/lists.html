About Lists

Article 16 of 22 in Using InteractInk

Lists let you group accounts together — by topic, by how well you know them, or however makes sense to you — and view a feed of just that group's posts.

  • Create a List from the Lists tab and give it a name.
  • Add any account to it from their profile, whether or not you follow them.
  • Set a List to private so only you can see it, or public so others can view and follow it too.

Viewing a List shows only posts from the accounts on it, in order, which is a handy way to keep tabs on a specific group without it getting lost in your main feed.
