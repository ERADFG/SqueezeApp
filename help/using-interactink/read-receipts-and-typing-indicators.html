Read receipts and typing indicators

Article 13 of 22 in Using InteractInk

InteractInk chats show two small but handy signals during a conversation:

Read receipts

Once the other person opens and views your message, you'll see a marker confirming it's been read — similar to how a lot of messaging apps handle it. In a group chat, this reflects each member individually.

Typing indicator

While someone is actively composing a reply, you'll briefly see a "typing…" indicator near the bottom of the chat, so you know a response is on its way.

Both of these are visible to everyone in a conversation and currently can't be turned off individually, so keep that in mind if you like to take your time replying.
