About end-to-end encrypted messages

Article 12 of 22 in Using InteractInk

Direct Messages on InteractInk are end-to-end encrypted, which means your messages are scrambled on your device before they're sent, and only unscrambled again on the recipient's device.

In practice, this means the content of your conversations can't be read as plain text by anyone in between, including in transit. Encryption keys are generated and stored on your own devices. If you sign in on a new device, that device needs to establish its own keys before it can decrypt your message history, which is why very old conversations can occasionally take a moment to sync on a freshly added device.

Encryption protects the content of your messages — it doesn't hide who you're messaging or when, since that metadata is still needed to deliver the conversation to the right accounts.
