How to follow and unfollow someone

Article 2 of 22 in Using InteractInk

Following an account means their posts start showing up in your Following feed, and they'll see you in their followers list.

To follow someone

  • Go to their profile and tap the Follow button, or tap Follow directly from a post in your feed.
  • If their account is private, your request goes into a pending state until they approve it.

To unfollow someone

  • Visit their profile and tap the button that now reads Following — it'll switch to Unfollow on hover or tap.
  • Confirm, and their posts stop appearing in your Following feed immediately.

Unfollowing someone doesn't notify them, and it doesn't remove your past likes or replies on their posts.
