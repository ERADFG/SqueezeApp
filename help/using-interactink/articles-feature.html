Writing and reading Articles

Article 17 of 22 in Using InteractInk

Articles are InteractInk's home for longer, more structured writing than a single post allows — think essays, guides, or write-ups with images woven throughout.

To write one

Open the Articles tab and select New Article. The editor supports headings, bold and italic text, links, and inline images, so you can format a piece properly rather than cramming it into a short post.

To read Articles

Published Articles show up in the Articles feed and can also appear as a card in your regular Home feed when someone you follow publishes one. Tap through to read the full piece on its own dedicated page.
