Writing and reading Articles

Article 17 of 22 in Using InteractInk

Articles are InteractInk's home for longer, more structured writing than a single post allows room for — think essays, guides, or write-ups with images woven throughout the text rather than crammed into a short caption.

To write one: open the Articles tab and select New Article. The editor supports headings, bold and italic text, links, and inline images, so you can format a longer piece properly rather than trying to squeeze it into the constraints of a normal post.

To read Articles: published pieces show up in the dedicated Articles feed, and can also appear as a card in your regular Home feed whenever someone you follow publishes one. Tapping through opens the full piece on its own dedicated page, formatted for a longer, more comfortable reading experience than a typical feed post.
