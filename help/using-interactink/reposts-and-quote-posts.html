Reposts and quote posts

Article 4 of 22 in Using InteractInk

Both reposts and quote posts share someone else's post with your own followers, but they work a little differently and are meant for different situations.

Repost — tap the repost icon under a post and confirm. The original post shows up on your profile and in your followers' feeds exactly as it was originally written, still fully credited to the original author, with nothing of yours added to it.

Quote post — tap the repost icon and choose Quote instead. This opens the composer with the original post attached below, so you can add your own commentary above it before sharing the combination with your followers.

You can undo a repost at any time by tapping the same icon again on that post and selecting Undo repost, which removes it from your profile and from your followers' feeds immediately, though it doesn't affect a quote post you may have made from the same original.
