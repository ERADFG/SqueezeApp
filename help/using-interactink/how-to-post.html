How to post on InteractInk

Article 1 of 22 in Using InteractInk

A post is the basic building block of InteractInk — a short update that can include text, images, video, or a poll, and it shows up in your followers' feeds as well as on your own profile once published.

  • Tap the compose button — the pencil icon on mobile, or the "Post" box at the top of your Home feed on desktop.
  • Type your text; posts have a character limit shown as a small countdown ring right next to the Post button as you type.
  • Optionally attach images or a video, add a GIF, or turn the whole post into a poll instead.
  • Tap Post to publish it right away.

Once it's live, your post appears at the top of your followers' Following feeds, and may also surface in other people's For You feeds if it starts genuinely getting engagement. You can attach up to four images, or a single video, to any one post, though not both media types at the same time.
