How to post on InteractInk

Article 1 of 22 in Using InteractInk

A post is the basic building block of InteractInk — a short update that can include text, images, video, or a poll, and shows up in your followers' feeds and on your profile.

To write a post

  • Tap the compose button (the pencil icon on mobile, or the "Post" box at the top of your Home feed on desktop).
  • Type your text. Posts have a character limit shown as a countdown ring next to the Post button.
  • Optionally attach images or a video, add a GIF, or turn the post into a poll.
  • Tap Post to publish it right away.

Once it's live, your post appears at the top of your followers' Following feeds and may also be shown in For You if people are engaging with it. You can attach up to four images, or one video, per post.
