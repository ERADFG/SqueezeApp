About your For You and Following feeds

Article 3 of 22 in Using InteractInk

Your Home feed has two tabs at the top: For You and Following.

Following

A simple, reverse-chronological stream of posts from the accounts you follow. If you want a feed that shows exactly what the people you chose to follow are posting, and nothing else, this is it.

For You

A broader mix that includes posts from accounts you follow plus posts InteractInk thinks you'll find interesting based on what you engage with — likes, replies, reposts, and the communities you're part of. It's meant to help you discover posts and accounts outside your existing network.

InteractInk remembers which tab you had open last, so it'll reopen there the next time you visit.
