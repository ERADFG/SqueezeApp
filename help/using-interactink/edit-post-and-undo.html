Editing a post and the undo timer

Article 6 of 22 in Using InteractInk

After you tap Post, InteractInk actually holds it for a few seconds behind the scenes before it's truly published, giving you a brief window to catch a typo or simply change your mind before anyone else sees it.

Undo — right after posting, you'll see a brief Undo option appear on screen. Tapping it cancels the post entirely before it ever becomes visible to anyone else.

Editing — once a post is fully live, you can still open it and choose Edit from its menu to fix wording. It's best used for small corrections — a typo, a broken link — rather than substantially changing the point of the post after the fact, since any replies and reposts made before your edit stay attached to the post exactly as it now reads, which can look confusing if the meaning has shifted significantly.
