How to delete a post

Article 7 of 22 in Using InteractInk

You're always in control of your own posts and can remove one from InteractInk whenever you decide you no longer want it up.

  • Open the post and tap the ··· menu in the corner.
  • Select Delete.
  • Confirm when asked, since this step can't be undone afterward.

Once deleted, the post disappears from your profile, from everyone's feeds who may have seen it, and from search results, essentially as if it were never posted. Any replies that were left underneath it stay up on their own as independent posts, but they'll now show that they were replying to a post that's since been removed, rather than simply vanishing along with the original. Deletion is permanent from InteractInk's side, so if you think you might want the text again later, it's worth copying it somewhere before you delete.
