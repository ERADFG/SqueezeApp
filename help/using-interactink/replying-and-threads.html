Replying and creating threads

Article 5 of 22 in Using InteractInk

Replies let you respond directly to a specific post, and threads let you post a connected series of your own posts on one topic.

To reply

Tap the reply icon under any post, write your response, and tap Reply. Your reply appears nested under the original post and notifies the author.

To start a thread

Write your first post, then tap the + that appears after posting to add another post connected to it. Keep adding posts to build out a longer thought — readers can follow the whole thread top to bottom without leaving the page.

Anyone can reply to a public post unless the author has limited who can reply to it.
