Sending Direct Messages

Article 10 of 22 in Using InteractInk

Direct Messages (DMs) are private, one-on-one conversations between you and another account.

  • Tap the message icon in your sidebar or on someone's profile.
  • Search for the person you want to message and select them.
  • Type your message and hit send — you can also attach images, GIFs, and voice notes.

By default, messages from accounts you don't follow may land in a separate requests view until you accept the conversation. You can turn off DMs from people you don't follow entirely from your privacy settings.
