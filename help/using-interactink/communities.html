About Communities

Article 14 of 22 in Using InteractInk

Communities are focused spaces built around a specific topic, fandom, or interest — smaller boards that sit alongside your main feed.

Anyone can browse a public Community's posts, but you'll need to join to post or reply inside it. Once you're a member, posts from that Community can appear in your For You feed alongside your regular content, and you'll get a dedicated feed for each Community you've joined from the Communities tab in your sidebar.

Some Communities are moderated more actively than the main site, with their own pinned rules set by the people who run them — worth a quick read before you start posting.
