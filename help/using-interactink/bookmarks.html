Saving posts with Bookmarks

Article 9 of 22 in Using InteractInk

Bookmarks let you privately save any post to come back to and read again later, without liking it, replying to it, or reposting it — nothing about saving a Bookmark is visible to anyone else.

  • Open the ··· menu on a post and choose Add to Bookmarks.
  • Find everything you've saved from the Bookmarks tab in your sidebar, gathered in one place.
  • Remove a saved post the same way, via Remove from Bookmarks, whenever you no longer need it.

Nobody else can see your Bookmarks list — not the original author of the post you saved, and not your own followers browsing your profile. It's an entirely private space attached to your account, which makes it a good spot for saving things like recipes, threads you want to reread, or posts you just want to revisit without publicly signaling that you're interested in them.
