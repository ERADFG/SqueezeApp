Creating and managing a Community

Article 15 of 22 in Using InteractInk

Anyone can start a Community around a topic they care about.

  • Go to the Communities tab and select Create Community.
  • Give it a name, a description, and, optionally, an avatar and banner image.
  • Choose whether it's open for anyone to join or requires approval.

As the creator, you're automatically a moderator, which lets you pin rules, remove posts that break them, and approve or remove members. You can add other members as moderators from the Community's settings to help share the workload as it grows.
