Creating an InteractInk account

Article 1 of 10 in Managing your account

You need an account to post, reply, follow, and like on InteractInk — browsing is open to everyone, but participating requires signing up.

  • Go to the sign-up page and enter your email address.
  • Choose a username — this is your public handle and can be changed later.
  • Set a password and confirm it.
  • Verify your email if prompted, then finish setting up your profile.

Once your account is created, you can add a profile picture, a bio, and start following people right away.
