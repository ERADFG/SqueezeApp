Changing your display language

Article 9 of 10 in Managing your account

InteractInk's interface is available in several languages, including English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Japanese, and Russian.

You can switch languages from the links in the site footer, or from your account language setting once you're logged in. This changes the site's menus, buttons, and static pages — it doesn't translate other people's posts, though you can translate an individual post from its options menu if it's written in a language you don't read.
