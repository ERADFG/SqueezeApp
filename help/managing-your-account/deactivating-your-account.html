Deactivating your account

Article 6 of 10 in Managing your account

Deactivating removes your profile, posts, and activity from public view without permanently deleting your data right away.

  • Go to Settings → Deactivate account.
  • Confirm you understand what deactivating does.

Once deactivated, your account disappears from search, profiles, and feeds. If you log back in during the grace period InteractInk gives you, your account reactivates automatically with everything intact. If that window passes without you logging back in, your account and its data may be permanently removed.
