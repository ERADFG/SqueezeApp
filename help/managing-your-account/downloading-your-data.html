Downloading your data

Article 7 of 10 in Managing your account

You can request a downloadable copy of your InteractInk activity at any time, which is useful whether you're just curious what's stored about you, backing things up before a big account change, or moving on from the platform entirely.

  • Go to Settings → Your data.
  • Request an archive of your account.
  • Once it's ready, you'll get a link to download it.

Putting the archive together can take anywhere from a few minutes to a while longer, depending on how much you've posted and how much media is attached to your account — a brand-new account with a handful of posts will be ready almost instantly, while a long-running, media-heavy account takes longer to package. The archive typically includes your posts, profile information, and core account details, giving you a self-contained snapshot of what's associated with your account rather than requiring you to dig through the site screen by screen.
