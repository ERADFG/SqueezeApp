Managing notification preferences

Article 8 of 10 in Managing your account

You don't have to get notified about everything. From Settings → Notifications, you can turn specific notification types on or off individually — for example, muting notifications for likes while keeping replies and mentions on.

These preferences apply across the notifications you see in-app. If notifications feel too noisy, start by trimming the lower-priority categories like likes and reposts first, and keep mentions and replies on so you don't miss direct conversation.
