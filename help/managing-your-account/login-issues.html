Trouble logging in

Article 10 of 10 in Managing your account

If you're stuck at the login screen, a few things are worth checking first:

  • Wrong password: use the Forgot password? link to reset it.
  • Wrong email or username: double-check for typos, extra spaces, or an old email address you've since changed.
  • Account locked or suspended: you'll see a message explaining why — check appealing a suspension if that's the case.
  • Browser issues: clear your cache and cookies, or try a different browser, if the page itself won't load properly.

Still stuck? Reach out through the Contact page with as much detail as you can about what's happening.
