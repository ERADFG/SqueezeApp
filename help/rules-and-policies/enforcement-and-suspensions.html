How enforcement and suspensions work

Article 7 of 10 in Rules and policies

Not every violation is treated the same way — enforcement scales with the severity and pattern of the behavior.

  • Minor or first-time issues often result in the specific post being removed, sometimes with a warning.
  • Repeated violations can lead to a temporary suspension, limiting posting or visibility for a set period.
  • Severe violations — illegal content, serious harassment, or child safety violations — result in immediate, permanent suspension, no warning required.

Whenever an account is actioned, you'll be shown which rule was violated and what the consequence is, along with your options for the next step.
