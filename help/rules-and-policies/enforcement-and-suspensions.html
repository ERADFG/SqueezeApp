How enforcement and suspensions work

Article 7 of 10 in Rules and policies

Not every rule violation on InteractInk is treated the same way — enforcement is meant to scale with both the severity of the behavior and whether it's a one-off mistake or part of a repeated pattern.

  • Minor or first-time issues often result in just the specific post being removed, sometimes accompanied by a warning rather than a suspension.
  • Repeated violations of the same kind can lead to a temporary suspension, limiting posting or visibility for a set period of time.
  • Severe violations — illegal content, serious harassment, or child safety violations — result in immediate, permanent suspension, with no warning required beforehand.

Whenever any action is taken against an account, you'll be shown exactly which specific rule was violated and what the resulting consequence is, along with a clear explanation of your available options for the next step, whether that's simply acknowledging the removal or filing a formal appeal if you believe the decision was made in error.
