Impersonation policy

Article 5 of 10 in Rules and policies

You can't pose as another person, brand, or organization in a way that's misleading. That means using someone's name, photo, and identity to deceive people into thinking your account is really theirs.

Parody, fan, and commentary accounts are fine, but they need to make clear in the account name or bio that they aren't the real person or organization — for example, labeling it "parody" or "fan account" plainly rather than burying that detail. If you find an account impersonating you, you can report it directly.
