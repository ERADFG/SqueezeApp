Abusive behavior and harassment policy

Article 3 of 10 in Rules and policies

Harassment covers repeated unwanted contact, threats, insults aimed at a specific person, and coordinated pile-ons meant to intimidate someone into silence. A single heated reply in a public disagreement is treated differently than a sustained pattern of targeting one account.

If you're being harassed, you don't have to deal with it alone — block or mute the accounts involved, and report the account so moderators can review the pattern of behavior, not just a single post.
