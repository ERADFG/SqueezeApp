Intellectual property and trademark

Article 9 of 10 in Rules and policies

Trademark complaints are different from copyright: they're about someone using a brand name, logo, or likeness in a way that could confuse people about who they're really dealing with — for example, an account using a company's branding to pretend to be that company's official presence.

If you believe your trademark is being misused this way, reach out through the Contact page with details of the account or post in question and a description of your trademark rights, and it'll be reviewed accordingly.
