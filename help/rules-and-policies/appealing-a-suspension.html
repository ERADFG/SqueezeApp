Appealing a suspension

Article 8 of 10 in Rules and policies

If you think a removal or suspension was a mistake, you can appeal it.

  • Open the notice explaining the action on your account — it includes an Appeal option.
  • Explain, clearly and specifically, why you believe the decision was incorrect.
  • Submit and wait for a human review — appeals aren't decided by the same automated process as the original action.

Appeals are reviewed as promptly as possible, though response times can vary. Submitting multiple duplicate appeals for the same action doesn't speed up the review.
