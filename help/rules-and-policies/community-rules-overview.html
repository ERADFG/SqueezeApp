InteractInk's community rules, explained

Article 1 of 10 in Rules and policies

InteractInk's rules are intentionally kept short, but here's what each one actually means in day-to-day practice on the platform:

  • No illegal content, ever. This includes child sexual abuse material without exception of any kind — violations are reported directly to law enforcement.
  • No doxxing. Don't post someone else's home address, phone number, or other private information without their explicit consent.
  • No spam or flooding. Repetitive, unwanted, or purely promotional posting isn't allowed on the platform.
  • No harassment or threats. Targeted abuse directed at another specific person isn't tolerated here.
  • Respect copyright. Don't upload media that you don't personally have the rights to share.
  • No impersonation or ban evasion. One account, honestly represented, per person using the platform.

You can read the complete, unabridged rules on the Rules & FAQ page. Having an account here is treated as a privilege rather than an unconditional right, and moderators can remove content or suspend accounts that break these rules, scaled to how serious and repeated the violation turns out to be.
