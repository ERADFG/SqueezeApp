InteractInk's community rules, explained

Article 1 of 10 in Rules and policies

InteractInk's rules are intentionally short, but here's what each one means in practice:

  • No illegal content, ever. This includes child sexual abuse material without exception — violations are reported to law enforcement.
  • No doxxing. Don't post someone else's address, phone number, or other private information without their consent.
  • No spam or flooding. Repetitive, unwanted, or purely promotional posting isn't allowed.
  • No harassment or threats. Targeted abuse of another person isn't tolerated.
  • Respect copyright. Don't upload media you don't have the rights to share.
  • No impersonation or ban evasion. One account, honestly represented, per person.

You can read the full rules on the Rules & FAQ page. Having an account is a privilege, and moderators can remove content or suspend accounts that break these rules.
