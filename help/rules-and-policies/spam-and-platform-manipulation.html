Spam and platform manipulation

Article 4 of 10 in Rules and policies

InteractInk doesn't allow behavior designed to artificially inflate reach or deceive other users about what's genuinely popular. This includes, but isn't limited to:

  • Posting the same content repeatedly, or across many accounts at once, purely to flood feeds or search results.
  • Buying or selling followers, likes, or reposts.
  • Running networks of fake or automated accounts specifically to manipulate what appears popular.
  • Aggressive, unsolicited following or messaging used purely as a promotional tactic.

Accounts found engaging in this kind of behavior can have their reach limited, have the artificial engagement removed from their posts, or be suspended entirely, depending on how severe and repeated the pattern turns out to be. A single borderline case is usually handled more gently than a clear, sustained campaign of manipulation, so context and history both factor into how any given report is resolved.
