InteractInk glossary

Article 2 of 6 in Resources

A few terms worth knowing as you get familiar with the site:

  • Post — a single piece of content you share, made up of text, media, or a poll.
  • Repost — sharing someone else's post to your own followers unchanged, credited to the original author.
  • Quote post — resharing a post with your own commentary added on top of it.
  • Thread — a connected chain of your own posts on one topic, readable top to bottom.
  • Community — a topic-focused space you can join, post in, and follow separately from your main feed.
  • Bookmark — a post you've privately saved to come back to and read again later.
  • For You / Following — the two tabs on your Home feed; one is algorithmically recommended content, the other is a strictly chronological stream of posts from accounts you follow.

If you come across a term elsewhere on InteractInk that isn't listed here and you're not sure what it means, the Contact page is always open for a quick question.
