How you can help keep InteractInk a good place to be

Article 5 of 6 in Resources

Moderation isn't only InteractInk's job to handle from behind the scenes — the community shapes what the site feels like too. A few things genuinely help keep it a good place to be:

  • Report, don't retaliate. If you see something that breaks the rules, report it rather than quote-posting it to pile on or start a public fight.
  • Use block and mute freely. Curating who you interact with isn't rude — it's a healthy, normal way to use a social platform.
  • Assume good faith in disagreements. Most heated moments online turn out to be misunderstandings rather than genuine malice.
  • Credit and ask permission before reposting someone's original art, writing, or media elsewhere.

InteractInk is built and maintained by a single developer rather than a large trust-and-safety team, so reports genuinely do get read by a real person rather than disappearing into an automated system — thank you for helping keep things running smoothly by reporting what needs it and letting the smaller stuff go.
