How your For You feed decides what to show you

Article 4 of 6 in Resources

Your For You feed isn't random — it's built from a mix of signals about what you've engaged with and what's happening across the accounts and Communities you're connected to.

  • Your own activity — the posts you like, reply to, and repost shape what gets recommended next.
  • Your network — accounts, Lists, and Communities you follow feed directly into what's surfaced.
  • Overall engagement — posts getting a lot of genuine interaction from people similar to you are more likely to appear.

None of this is set in stone — the more you like, reply to, and follow accounts you actually enjoy, the more your For You feed reflects that over time. If you'd rather see a feed with none of this involved, switch to the Following tab for a purely chronological view.
