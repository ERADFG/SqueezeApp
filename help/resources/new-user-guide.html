New to InteractInk? Start here

Article 1 of 6 in Resources

Welcome to InteractInk. If you just made an account and aren't sure what to do first, here's the fastest way to get oriented:

  • Fill out your profile — a photo and a short bio make it much easier for people to know who they're actually talking to.
  • Follow a few accounts in topics you're interested in to seed your Following feed with content you'll actually want to see.
  • Check out Communities for spaces built around a specific interest, fandom, or hobby.
  • Write your first post — it doesn't need to be perfect, just yours.

From there, everything else on the platform — replies, reposts, direct messages, Lists — builds naturally the more you use the site, and none of it needs to be figured out all at once. If you'd like the full rundown of what is and isn't allowed before you start posting, it's worth taking a few minutes to read through the community rules, since knowing the boundaries up front tends to make the whole experience smoother.
