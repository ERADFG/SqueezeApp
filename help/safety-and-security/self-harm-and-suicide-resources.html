Self-harm and suicide: how InteractInk responds

Article 10 of 10 in Safety and security

If you or someone you know is struggling, please reach out to a crisis line in your country — in the US you can call or text 988, and in many other countries a local equivalent is available.

InteractInk doesn't allow content that promotes or encourages self-harm or suicide. If you come across a post that concerns you — either because it's promoting self-harm or because someone appears to be in crisis — please report it so it can be reviewed, rather than sharing or amplifying it further.
