Self-harm and suicide: how InteractInk responds

Article 10 of 10 in Safety and security

If you or someone you know is struggling right now, please reach out to a crisis line in your country. In the United States, you can call or text 988 to reach the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline. Many other countries have a local equivalent, and a quick search for "crisis line" plus your country's name will usually turn up the right number for where you are.

InteractInk does not allow content that promotes, encourages, or provides instructions for self-harm or suicide. If you come across a post that concerns you on the platform — whether it's actively promoting self-harm to others, or it seems like someone posting it may themselves be in crisis — please use the report tool so it can be reviewed by a person, rather than replying to it publicly, quote-posting it, or otherwise amplifying it further, which can sometimes make things worse for everyone involved.
