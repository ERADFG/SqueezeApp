Blocking accounts

Article 1 of 10 in Safety and security

Blocking stops another account from seeing your posts, following you, or messaging you.

  • Go to the account's profile, tap the ··· menu, and select Block.
  • Confirm the block.

Once blocked, they can't view your profile or posts while logged in, follow you, or send you a DM, and you'll stop seeing their content too. Blocking is silent — the other account isn't notified. You can undo it from the same menu at any time by selecting Unblock.
