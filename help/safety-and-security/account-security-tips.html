Account security tips

Article 6 of 10 in Safety and security

A handful of simple habits go a long way toward keeping your account yours and out of anyone else's hands:

  • Use a unique password for InteractInk that you don't reuse on any other site.
  • Never share your password or a login link someone sends you, even if the message looks official or urgent.
  • Double-check the URL before entering your login details — it should always be interactink.vercel.app, nothing else.
  • Log out of shared or public devices as soon as you're finished using them.

If something ever feels off with your account — posts showing up that you didn't write, settings that have changed without you touching them, or notifications about a login you don't recognize — reset your password immediately, even before investigating further, and then review your recent account activity to see what may have happened while you regain full control.
