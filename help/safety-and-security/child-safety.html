Child safety on InteractInk

Article 9 of 10 in Safety and security

Content that sexually exploits or endangers children has absolutely zero tolerance on InteractInk. This includes any child sexual abuse material, grooming behavior directed at minors, and any content that sexualizes minors in any way — there are no exceptions carved out for content labeled as fictional, drawn, or "just art," and none for content someone claims is satire.

Violations of this policy result in an immediate, permanent account suspension with no warning and no appeal process for that specific decision, and they are reported to the relevant authorities, including the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and law enforcement, where applicable under law. If you come across this kind of content anywhere on InteractInk, please report it right away through the standard reporting tools rather than engaging with it, replying to it, downloading it, or sharing it further in any way — doing so, even with good intentions, can itself cause additional harm and complicate the response.
