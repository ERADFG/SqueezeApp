Sensitive media settings

Article 7 of 10 in Safety and security

Some posts are marked as containing sensitive media — graphic or adult content that not everyone wants to see by default.

These posts appear behind a warning that you have to tap through before the media loads. You can adjust whether you see this content at all, and whether your own posts get flagged this way, from your content settings. If you're posting media that falls into this category yourself, mark it as sensitive when composing so others get the same warning before viewing it.
