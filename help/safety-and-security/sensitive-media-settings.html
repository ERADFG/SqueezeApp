Sensitive media settings

Article 7 of 10 in Safety and security

Some posts on InteractInk are marked as containing sensitive media — graphic imagery, adult content, or other material that not everyone wants to see appear automatically while scrolling their feed.

Posts flagged this way appear behind a warning label that you have to deliberately tap through before the actual image or video loads, so nothing sensitive surfaces unexpectedly. You can adjust how this works for your own account from your content settings: whether you want to see this kind of content at all, and separately, whether your own posts get automatically flagged this way based on what you attach. If you're posting media that falls into this category yourself, it's good practice to mark it as sensitive at the time you compose the post, rather than relying on automatic detection, so that everyone who sees it gets the same warning before the media actually loads on their screen.
