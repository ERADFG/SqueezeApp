Reporting a post

Article 3 of 10 in Safety and security
  • Open the ··· menu on the post you want to report.
  • Select Report post.
  • Choose the reason that best matches the issue — spam, harassment, sensitive content, and so on.
  • Submit the report.

Reports go into a moderation queue that isn't publicly visible, and you won't see who else has reported the same post. You don't need to be following someone, or even have an account interaction with them, to report their post.
