Recognizing phishing and fake emails

Article 8 of 10 in Safety and security

Phishing attempts often imitate InteractInk to trick you into handing over your password. A few signs to watch for:

  • Urgent language pushing you to log in immediately or risk losing your account.
  • Links that don't actually point to interactink.vercel.app when you hover over them.
  • Requests for your password sent directly by email or DM — InteractInk will never ask for it that way.

If you receive something suspicious, don't click the link. Go to the site directly by typing the address yourself, and report the message if it came through InteractInk's own messaging.
