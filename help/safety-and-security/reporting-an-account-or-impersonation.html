Reporting an account or impersonation

Article 4 of 10 in Safety and security

If the problem is with an account as a whole rather than a single post — repeated rule-breaking, spam behavior, or impersonation — you can report the account directly.

  • Go to the account's profile and open the ··· menu.
  • Select Report account.
  • Pick the reason, and choose Impersonation if the account is pretending to be you or someone you represent.

For impersonation specifically, having a couple of examples ready — links to the fake account and something proving your identity — helps the review move faster.
