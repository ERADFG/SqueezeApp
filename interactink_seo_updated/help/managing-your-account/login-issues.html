Trouble logging in

Article 10 of 10 in Managing your account

Getting stuck at the login screen is frustrating, but most cases come down to one of a handful of common causes. Here's what to check, roughly in order of how often each one turns out to be the culprit:

  • Wrong password — use the Forgot password? link to reset it rather than guessing repeatedly.
  • Wrong email or username — double-check for typos, extra spaces, or an old email address you've since replaced.
  • Account locked or suspended — you'll see a message explaining why; if that's the case, see Appealing a suspension.
  • Browser issues — clear your cache and cookies, or try a different browser, if the login page itself won't load or behaves strangely.

If you've worked through all of that and you're still stuck, reach out through the Contact page with as much detail as you can provide about exactly what's happening — the error message you see, what device and browser you're using, and roughly when the problem started. That context makes it much faster to figure out what's actually going wrong on your particular account.
